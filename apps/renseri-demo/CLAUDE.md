# Vaskeri- og renserivirksomhet — Demo Website

## Your task
Build a complete, production-quality demo website for a Norwegian Vaskeri- og renserivirksomhet business.
This is a cold outreach demo — it must look real, feel premium, and be unique to this industry.
All copy in Norwegian Bokmål.

## IMPORTANT: Research first, then design
Before writing any code:
1. Use WebFetch to fetch and study these real Norwegian sites in this industry:
www.asvo-kvinesdal.no
www.aufera.no
www.boproas.no
2. Analyze what makes them work: layout patterns, section ordering, what information customers care about, how they present services, what CTAs they use
3. Design YOUR site based on what you learn — each industry has unique needs and conventions. Do NOT use a generic business template layout.

## Technical approach
- Single page.tsx file with all content
- "use client" with useState/useEffect for interactivity
- Tailwind v4 with @theme tokens in globals.css
- Two Google Fonts that suit the industry aesthetic
- lucide-react icons
- All hardcoded Norwegian content — no CMS
- Use the impeccable:frontend-design skill for design quality

## After building
Run: npm run build
Fix any errors until build passes. Do not deploy.

## What laundry/dry cleaning sites typically need
- Service list with pricing (rens, vask, stryking, skinnbehandling, gardiner, bunader)
- Pickup/delivery service info
- Turnaround times
- "Bestill henting" / "Lever inn" as primary CTA
- Price transparency
- Business/hotel services (B2B)
- Opening hours and drop-off locations

## Design direction
**Aesthetic:** Clean, fresh, efficient. Simple, trustworthy, no-fuss.
**Color palette:** Light blue #dbeafe, white #ffffff, clean green #16a34a, charcoal #2e2e2e
